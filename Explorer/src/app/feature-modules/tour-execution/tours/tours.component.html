🔍 Tour Search

This window allows you to easily find the perfect tour based on your preferences. Here's how it works:

1

Search by Location

You can search for tours within a specific radius from your current location. This is an optional filter that helps you find tours nearby, making your adventure more accessible.

2

Adventure Coins

Use your Adventure Coins as a currency to purchase selected tours. Accumulate coins through your adventures and spend them on new experiences!

3

Coupons & Discounts

If you have any active coupons, you can apply them during checkout to receive additional discounts on your selected tours.

4

Tour Bundles

If you're interested in buying a package of tours, you’ll need to do that in the Browse Bundles window. Bundles allow you to purchase multiple tours at once for a better deal.

5

Souvenirs

For the souvenir lovers, you can browse and buy souvenirs in the Browse Souvenirs window. It's the perfect way to remember your adventures.

⚠️ Important: You cannot purchase the same tour more than once unless you’ve completed it. Make sure to finish the tour before purchasing it again!

Once you’ve purchased a tour, you can start it from your Purchased Tours window. From there, you’ll follow the map through all the checkpoints, uncover secrets, and enjoy the full adventure experience!
