🎟 How to Create a Coupon?

Creating a coupon is quick and easy! Here's how:

1

Discount Type

Choose whether the coupon applies to a specific tour or all tours created by you.

2

Discount Percentage

Select the percentage of the discount you'd like to offer.

3

Coupon Code

The system will generate a unique code consisting of 8 random letters.

4

Sharing

Once your coupon is created, you can share it with anyone!

5

Redemption

Users can apply the coupon code during their booking to receive the discount.

It's that simple! Share your coupon and give your tours an extra boost! 🎉
