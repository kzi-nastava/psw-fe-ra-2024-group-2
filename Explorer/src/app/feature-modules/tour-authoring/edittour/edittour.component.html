🏞 Tour Management

This window is designed specifically for managing the selected tour from your list. Here, you can:

1

Edit Tour Details

Modify the name, description, price, and choose which equipment is used or not used for this tour.

2

View Tour Information

See all details related to the tour, including:

  • Map of Checkpoints – View and adjust the map of your checkpoints.
  • Difficulty Level – Check or modify the difficulty rating of your tour.
  • Tags – Review and update any tags (e.g., historical, adventure, nature).
  • Equipment – See which equipment is currently associated with the tour and select which equipment is used or not used.
3

Publish or Archive

Once you're satisfied, you can:

  • Publish the tour to make it live.
  • Archive the tour if it needs further adjustments or doesn't meet the requirements.

🔒 Important: To publish your tour, the price must be greater than 0. Be sure to set a price before publishing!

That's it! Manage your tours easily and share your experiences with the world! 🌍✨
